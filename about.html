My Story

How Flower Bloom Studios started.

I'm a business marketing student at Louisiana State University, right here in Baton Rouge, but my start in design goes back a little further. It began a couple of years ago when I decided to create my own clothing brand. Building that brand meant learning how to make something look good and actually get people to care about it, and I found out I genuinely loved that part.

To get the brand online, I built myself a website. Then a friend asked me to make one for them, and then someone in my family, and then another. Every time, the same thing happened: a business that did great work but had nothing online to show for it suddenly looked as good as it actually was.

That is when it clicked. There are so many hard working companies, especially the home service businesses that keep a place running, losing jobs to competitors with a nicer website rather than better work. So I created Flower Bloom Studios to fix exactly that. Studying marketing at LSU sharpens the part that matters most to you: a website is only doing its job if it actually brings in customers, and that is what I build every site to do. I want to help your business grow too.

What that means for you

Because it is just me, you get something an agency cannot really offer: you work directly with the person doing the work, from the first call all the way through launch and every text after. No account managers, no red tape, no being passed around.

Every site is custom built from scratch around your business, quoted at one flat price up front, and live in as little as two weeks. After launch you own everything, your domain, your hosting, your site, and you have my number if you ever need anything.
